Municipality Email Maps

Who runs the official email of your municipality, and how well is it protected against fake senders? Interactive maps of ~15,300 municipalities in Germany, Austria, and Switzerland, based on public DNS records and network signals. They accompany our peer-reviewed SecAssure 2026 paper.

Which provider handles each municipality's official email? Classified by jurisdiction: US cloud (Microsoft, Google, AWS), domestic, or foreign.
